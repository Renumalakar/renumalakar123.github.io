OUR STORY

Making Childhood More Colorful

Welcome to Renu Toys, a business dedicated to bringing fun, happiness and memorable moments to children.

Our business was established in 1992 by Renu Malakar. With years of experience, we believe that a good toy is more than just something to play with — it can inspire creativity, imagination and learning.

We are committed to offering a variety of enjoyable toys suitable for children of different ages and interests.
